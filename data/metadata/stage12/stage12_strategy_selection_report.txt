1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & DECISION POLICY
--------------------------------------------------
Status Policy: SHORTLIST ESTABLISHED (NO SINGLE WINNER DECLARED)
Upstream Scope Audited: Stages 5–11 completed and validated without error.
Scientific Rule: A preprocessing method cannot be declared 'optimal' based solely
on human visual preference, high synthetic PSNR, or edge gradient magnitude.
An image modification that appears sharp or high-contrast to human observers may
introduce high-frequency noise artifacts that impair convolutional neural network
(CNN) generalization. Conversely, non-linear denoising risks smoothing out
subtle punctate microcalcifications and spiculated tumor borders.
Therefore, Stage 12 establishes a scientifically defensible candidate shortlist (C1–C5)
to carry forward into Stage 13 (Classification Dataset Preparation), where downstream
model training (Stages 14–17) will empirically determine true diagnostic efficacy.

2. PREPROCESSING CANDIDATE SHORTLIST FOR STAGE 13
--------------------------------------------------
[C1_BASELINE] Baseline Preprocessing (Control) (Baseline)
  Role:         Mandatory Scientific Control Reference
  Source:       Stage 5 Baseline Preprocessing (N=16 pilot, 3568 corpus)
  Key Metrics:  Entropy=4.6143 bits, Mean Grad=9.45, Strong Edges=10.8%
  Benefits:     Provides an uncorrupted benchmark for deep learning feature extraction. Guarantees that classification models do not learn preprocessing-induced artifacts.
  Risks:        Dense glandular regions (BI-RADS density C/D) exhibit lower local contrast, potentially challenging early-stage feature extraction in shallow networks.
  Evidence Gap: Effect on deep CNN convolutional feature activations must be quantified empirically in Stage 14.

[C2_CLAHE] Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE) (Contrast Enhancement)
  Role:         Primary Contrast Enhancement Candidate
  Source:       Stage 6 Contrast Enhancement Experiment (N=16 pilot, 16 corpus)
  Key Metrics:  Entropy=4.9258 bits, Mean Grad=16.08, Strong Edges=26.44%
  Benefits:     Highest entropy (4.93 bits) among contrast methods; boosts subtle border visibility in dense tissue, which may facilitate mass margin discrimination in CNNs.
  Risks:        May subtly elevate high-frequency sensor noise in low-density adipose regions; tile boundaries could theoretically produce microscopic gradient discontinuities.
  Evidence Gap: Tested on 16 pilot mammograms; generalizability across the full 3,568 corpus and impact on classification false-positive rates must be tested in Stages 13–17.

[C3_UNSHARP_MASK] High-Frequency Sharpening (Unsharp Masking) (Edge Enhancement)
  Role:         Primary Edge Enhancement Candidate
  Source:       Stage 7 Sharpening Experiment (N=16 pilot, 16 corpus)
  Key Metrics:  Entropy=4.7529 bits, Mean Grad=14.02, Strong Edges=21.98%
  Benefits:     Accentuates high-frequency spatial transitions critical for microcalcification cluster and subtle spiculated malignant lesion detection.
  Risks:        Indiscriminate high-frequency gain sharpens background quantum mottle and scanner noise, which may lead CNN feature extractors to attend to spurious noise spikes.
  Evidence Gap: Whether edge sharpening increases true positive sensitivity or simply elevates false positives on normal fibroglandular parenchyma remains unknown prior to Stage 14.

[C4_CLAHE_UNSHARP] Compound Enhancement (CLAHE + Unsharp Masking) (Compound Enhancement)
  Role:         Exploratory Compound Candidate
  Source:       Stage 7 Factorial Sharpening Experiment (Condition: clahe + unsharp_mask) (N=16 pilot, 16 corpus)
  Key Metrics:  Entropy=4.9748 bits, Mean Grad=24.25, Strong Edges=36.15%
  Benefits:     Maximizes visual conspicuity by simultaneously expanding local dynamic range and sharpening fine edges.
  Risks:        Compounding risk: CLAHE local noise enhancement is subsequently sharpened by unsharp masking. Highest mean absolute change from baseline (1.32 intensity levels; max diff 111.0).
  Evidence Gap: Must be validated downstream to ensure CNN models are not distracted by intensified texture patterns.

[C5_MEDIAN_DENOISING] Non-Linear Denoising (Median Filtering, 3x3) (Denoising)
  Role:         Exploratory Denoising Candidate (Noise Robustness Benchmark)
  Source:       Stages 8–10 Denoising Benchmark (Comparison B & C) (N=16 pilot, 80 corpus)
  Key Metrics:  Entropy=4.7998 bits, Mean Grad=0.0753, Strong Edges=nan%
                Stage 10 PSNR=36.06 dB, SSIM=0.8037
  Benefits:     Demonstrated top general restoration (36.06 dB PSNR, 0.8037 SSIM) in Stage 10 benchmark; serves as a controlled candidate to evaluate whether noise suppression helps CNN generalization.
  Risks:        Critical Clinical Risk: Denoising raw, uncorrupted clinical scans risks blurring subtle, punctate microcalcifications (< 0.2 mm) and fine spicules, potentially destroying life-saving diagnostic cues. Stage 10 evaluations evaluated synthetic noise, not native scanner noise.
  Evidence Gap: Denoising was benchmarked against artificial synthetic noise. Its utility on clinical scans without added noise is unproven and must be compared directly against Baseline (C1) in Stage 14.

3. REJECTED / DISCARDED PREPROCESSING STRATEGIES
--------------------------------------------------
[DISC_01_HISTEQ] Global Histogram Equalization (HistEq) (Contrast Enhancement)
  Evidence Basis: Stage 6 Contrast Experiment & Stage 11 Visual Panels
  Reason:         Severely expands global dynamic range across non-breast background areas, amplifying sensor noise. Causes excessive saturation and whiteout in dense fibroglandular parenchyma, obliterating fine internal mass structures. Entropy decreased from 4.6143 to 4.5282 bits.

[DISC_02_GAUSSIAN_BLUR] Gaussian Spatial Smoothing (Denoising) (Denoising)
  Evidence Basis: Stage 10 Quantitative Summary & Stage 11 Magnified Crops
  Reason:         Indiscriminate low-pass spatial filtering washes out fine structural details and smooths out microcalcifications. Mean gradient dropped to 0.0642 (lowest of all methods). Inferior SSIM (0.7521) to median filtering.

[DISC_03_NLM] Non-Local Means (NLM Denoising) (Denoising)
  Evidence Basis: Stage 10 Quantitative Summary & Stage 9 Runtime Logs
  Reason:         Extreme computational complexity (~10-15 seconds per high-resolution mammogram, requiring > 10 hours for 3,568 images). Low restoration fidelity under Comparison B (PSNR 27.88 dB, SSIM 0.6349), indicating aggressive alteration of native tissue.

[DISC_04_ADAPTIVE_MEDIAN] Adaptive Median Filtering (Denoising)
  Evidence Basis: Stage 10 Summary by Method & Noise Type
  Reason:         Highly specialized for salt-and-pepper noise (PSNR 52.77 dB), but inferior to standard median filtering on continuous Gaussian, Poisson, and Speckle noise models (overall PSNR 33.82 dB vs 36.06 dB for standard median).

[DISC_05_UNTESTED_CHAINS] Arbitrary Multi-Stage Pipelines (e.g. Denoise -> HistEq -> Sharpen) (Arbitrary Chains)
  Evidence Basis: Strict Experimental Boundary Constraints
  Reason:         Scientific boundary: Chaining operations that were never tested in Stages 5–11 violates reproducible research protocols. Only factorial combinations explicitly tested in Stage 7 (CLAHE + Unsharp Mask) are eligible for shortlisting.

4. SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE SYNTHESIS & IDENTIFIED EVIDENCE GAPS
--------------------------------------------------
A. Contrast Enhancement:
   - Global HistEq is objectively detrimental due to background noise blowout and over-saturation.
   - CLAHE (clip=2.0) demonstrates superior entropy (4.93 bits) and clean glandular contrast.
B. Sharpening:
   - Unsharp masking (sigma=1.0, amount=1.0) doubles strong edge percentage (10.8% -> 22.0%).
   - Risk of sensor noise amplification requires evaluation against Baseline (C1) in Stage 14.
C. Denoising:
   - Median filtering (3x3) achieved highest overall restoration (PSNR 36.06 dB, SSIM 0.8037).
   - However, raw CBIS-DDSM mammograms lack severe synthetic noise; denoising uncorrupted scans
     risks blunting fine microcalcifications and tumor spicules.
D. Evidence Gaps:
   1. Aligned Lesion CNR: Unaligned full-mammogram ROI masks prevent meaningful CNR measurement.
   2. Classification Transferability: No pilot metric proves whether CNN feature maps benefit
      from edge acutance vs raw baseline gradients.
   3. Pilot Generalizability: Contrast and sharpening metrics were computed on 16 pilot mammograms;
      Stage 13 will expand evaluation across the full 3,568 corpus.
