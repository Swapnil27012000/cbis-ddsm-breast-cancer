1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This report synthesizes the completed 18-stage deep learning pipeline for binary breast
cancer classification (Benign vs. Malignant) on the CBIS-DDSM dataset.

Key Findings:
1. No Universal Model Winner:
   - DenseNet-121 achieved highest Sensitivity (81.71%), F1-Score (64.02%), and ROC-AUC (0.7479).
   - ResNet-50 achieved highest Accuracy (67.39%) and Specificity (70.28%).
   - EfficientNet-B0 achieved lowest BCE log-loss (0.61141) with only 4.01M parameters (83% fewer than ResNet-50).
   - Baseline CNN achieved 59.32% accuracy and 0.6670 ROC-AUC, demonstrating that deep
     transfer learning backbones provide significant discriminative gains.

2. Statistical Significance (McNemar Paired Discordance, k=6 comparisons, alpha=0.05):
   - ResNet-50 vs. Baseline CNN is statistically significant under both Bonferroni (p_adj = 0.0063)
     and Holm step-down (p_adj = 0.0063).
   - EfficientNet-B0 vs. Baseline CNN is statistically significant under Holm step-down (p_adj = 0.0478),
     though narrowly non-significant under Bonferroni (p_adj = 0.0574).
   - DenseNet-121 vs. Baseline CNN is not statistically significant (p_Holm = 0.2672).
   - No pairwise comparisons among the three transfer learning models are statistically
     significant (p > 0.05).

3. Methodological Rectification & Zero Leakage:
   - 31 cross-partition patient conflicts embedded in official CBIS-DDSM manifests (143 records)
     were quarantined in quarantined_conflict_manifest.csv.
   - Test partition comprises strictly 644 records from 318 unique patients with 100% isolation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. METHODOLOGICAL DISCLOSURES & THREATS TO VALIDITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Prior Test Exposure: Baseline CNN test metrics were evaluated in Stage 14 earlier
   in the project. Checkpoint selection was conducted strictly on validation loss.
2. Baseline Discrepancy: Historical Stage 14 evaluation recorded TN=197, FP=190.
   Stage 17.1 recorded TN=196, FP=191. Case P_00457_mass_LEFT_MLO_1 (logit +0.000583,
   prob 0.500146) shifted across the 0.50 threshold due to intermediate CUDA AMP FP16
   vs FP32 accumulation rounding. Stage 14 did not save a per-record CSV.
3. Severe Downsampling: Downscaling to 256x256 enables training on 4GB VRAM but loses
   sub-millimeter microcalcification morphological detail.
4. Single-Domain Film Data: Dataset reflects digitized film mammography from the 1990s.
   External generalization to modern FFDM or DBT cannot be assumed.
5. Not Clinically Validated: Experimental research prototype. Not for patient diagnosis.
